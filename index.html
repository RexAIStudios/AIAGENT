More than a coding assistant

Your desktop.
Your workflow.

AI AGENT brings project help, desktop actions, voice interaction, and personalization into one workspace. Available actions depend on your configuration and permissions.

⌘

Project-aware coding

Work with project folders, explore files, understand unfamiliar code, investigate errors, and make improvements with AI assistance.

◎

Web browsing

Browse the web to look up documentation, explore websites, and gather information while working on a task.

▣

Desktop actions

Open applications, launch Steam games, and press keyboard keys to help carry out supported desktop workflows.

↗

Account Manager

Use Account Manager to assist with signing in to websites. Keep your account credentials private and review actions involving your accounts.

✦

Memory & personalization

Memory can retain useful details, with custom personalization to help tailor future interactions to your preferences and workflow.

⚙

Settings that fit you

Configure available options to shape how AI AGENT behaves and how you prefer to work.

SelfComputerUse · Desktop Mode

AI assistance, right where you are.

SelfComputerUse and Desktop Mode are designed to help connect instructions with on-screen work, so you can interact with your desktop without constantly switching contexts.

⌖

Understand the app under your cursor

In Desktop Mode, double-tap the middle mouse button to bring up an overview of the app you're hovering over.

▤

Quick chat by your cursor

Triple-tap the middle mouse button to open a small chat window near your cursor for quick, contextual help.

⌁

Voice interaction

Use your microphone with a custom wake word, and dictate output when supported by your configuration for a more hands-free workflow.

The story behind it

Built out of a real coding frustration.

AI AGENT started with a familiar problem: working through PC projects, running into errors, and wanting help without hitting a chat limit at the wrong moment.

That frustration turned into a project of its own. AI AGENT is an independent effort to make an approachable coding companion available to more people.

AI AGENT is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI, Codex, or any other third-party product unless explicitly stated.
